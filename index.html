About Us

St. Anthony’s Child & Youth Care Centre is a registered centre in Blaauwbosch, near Newcastle, KwaZulu-Natal. Since 1986 we have given information, care, training and support to children and families facing circumstances that put a child’s safety, protection and development at risk.

Each year, about 100 children live with us. They receive guidance, a safe and clean home, medical care, education, acceptance and love. We also work with their families so children can return home safely, or move to another suitable family setting.

Our vision

Children and families receive quality care and develop holistically in a conducive and caring environment.

Our mission

To provide care, support and guidance, and to facilitate protection for vulnerable children and their families in KwaZulu-Natal.

Our main goal: keeping children safe within families.

Our Journey

  1. 1986–2004

    Set up orientation procedures, routine in-service training and team meetings. Began recognising and encouraging each child’s unique talents.

  2. 2003–2012

    Intensified family reunification work, grew funding and assets, professionalised the Child Care Team and adopted strategic planning.

  3. 2013–2019

    Improved staff care, celebrated achievements of children, families and staff, strengthened family preservation, prioritised individual development plans and improved access to higher education.

Our Programmes

We care for children from birth to 18 years, supporting their physical, cognitive, educational, spiritual, cultural and life-skills development, and their sense of identity.

  • Residential Care

    A safe home for children.

  • Educational

    Support for children’s schooling.

  • Development

    Support for holistic child development.

  • Therapeutic

    Support for emotional and psychological wellbeing.

  • Recreational

    Recreational activities for children.

  • Reunification & Reintegration

    Helping children reconnect with their families and communities.

  • Aftercare

    Support for young people beyond residential care.
